없는 분류 FK

NOT NULL
SQL: INSERT INTO member VALUES (999,NULL,'new@example.test','2026-10-01')
예상된 거절: NOT NULL constraint failed: member.name

UNIQUE
SQL: INSERT INTO member VALUES (999,'검사','member01@example.test','2026-10-01')
예상된 거절: UNIQUE constraint failed: member.email

PK 중복
SQL: INSERT INTO member VALUES (1,'검사','new@example.test','2026-10-01')
예상된 거절: UNIQUE constraint failed: member.id

음수 연체료
SQL: INSERT INTO rental VALUES (999,1,1,'2026-10-01',NULL,-1)

반납일 역전
